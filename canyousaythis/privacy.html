Can You Say This?

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Scope

This policy applies to “Can You Say This?” (the “App”), a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel “Can You Say This?” to publish and manage videos on that channel.

Google account data the App accesses

The App is authorized only by the channel owner’s own Google account, through Google OAuth, with the following scopes:

  • youtube / youtube.force-ssl — to upload videos to the owner’s channel, update their titles, descriptions, captions and thumbnails, add them to the owner’s playlists, and post and read comments on the owner’s own videos.
  • youtube.readonly — to confirm that uploads go to the intended channel and to check the processing status of uploaded videos.
  • yt-analytics.readonly — to read view counts and audience retention for the owner’s own videos, so the owner can see which videos perform well.

The App does not access any other person’s Google account.

Public comments on the channel’s videos

The App may read public comments that viewers post on the channel’s own videos, only to find words that viewers suggest for future videos. The comment text and the commenter’s public display name are read only while the check runs. The App keeps only the IDs of comments it has already checked, so that the same comment is not processed twice, and the suggested word itself if a video is made from it. It does not store comment text, viewers’ names or any other information about viewers.

How data is used and stored

The OAuth credentials (refresh token) are stored encrypted in an environment controlled by the owner and are used only to operate the owner’s channel as described above. Upload history (video IDs, titles and the generated words) is kept so the App does not repeat itself. No data is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, and no data is used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The App’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access granted to the App can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party access. Information on how Google handles data is available in the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please use the contact information on the “Can You Say This?” YouTube channel’s About page.
