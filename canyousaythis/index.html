Impossible words. Weird voices.

Can You Say This?

Can You Say This? is a YouTube channel of short videos that take impossible, glitched-out words and try to pronounce them anyway — with robot voices, backwards speech, choirs, Morse code and more. None of the words are real; it’s just for fun.

About this application

“Can You Say This?” is a personal tool used only by the channel’s owner to generate videos and publish them to the owner’s own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public: there is no sign-up, and no other user can sign in to it or connect their account.
